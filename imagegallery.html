About Aperture

Started with one photograph. Grown into a visual archive people return to.

Aperture began as a small personal archive — a place to keep the photographs worth revisiting. Over time it grew into a curated journal spanning landscapes, architecture, portraiture, and the quieter, in-between moments that don't always make it into a typical gallery.

Every collection here is chosen, not just uploaded — sorted by mood and subject so you can search for exactly what you're picturing, whether that's a single word like "coast" or a whole feeling like "quiet morning light."

We add new collections regularly. If you're a photographer and want your work considered for a future collection, reach out — we're always looking for new perspectives.
